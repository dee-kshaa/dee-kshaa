## ◈ About

Electronics and Communication Engineering student at CMRIT Bengaluru (VTU), focused on AI-enabled embedded systems, signal processing, and satellite navigation. My work is centered on GNSS/SBAS analysis, intelligent sensing workflows, and research-driven engineering that connects theory with deployable technical systems.

I am particularly interested in aviation navigation support systems, embedded AI, and reliable data-to-decision pipelines for real-world environments.

**Open to:** Research internships, graduate research opportunities (MS/PhD), and engineering roles in signal processing, embedded systems, and satellite navigation.
